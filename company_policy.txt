
Galaxy Corp Internal Policy (2026 Edition)

1. Dress Code
All employees must wear standard-issue silver jumpsuits on "Future Fridays".
Regular business casual is permitted Monday through Thursday.

2. Security Protocols
- Password rotation is mandatory every 42 days.
- Do not feed the server room hamsters after midnight.
- Data breaches must be reported to the AI Sentinel within 30 seconds.

3. Remote Work
Remote work is allowed from any planet in the local solar system, provided latency is under 500ms.
Mars colony employees must adjust their clocks to Earth time for meetings.
